ма́-ма — syllables; the accent (á) marks the stressed syllable: say it louder and longer. [mama] — the sound in English letters. ماما — the sound in Arabic letters (پ = п, ڤ = в, گ = г). Unstressed о sounds like а. 🐢 plays slowly; ▶▶ plays the whole page.
